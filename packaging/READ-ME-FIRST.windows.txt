OnePaste on Windows: allow it to open the first time
====================================================

OnePaste is not signed, so Windows may warn you the first time you open it.
This is expected. To allow it:

1. Double-click OnePaste.exe.
2. If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then click
   Run anyway.

Nothing is installed and no administrator rights are needed. You only have to
do this once.

OnePaste is an early beta. It can keep a searchable history of what you copy and,
if you connect it to your own server, sync it between your computers. See the
release page for what works, what does not yet, and how to remove it completely.
