OnePaste on macOS: allow it to open the first time
==================================================

OnePaste is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS blocks it the first
time you open it. This is expected, and the app is not damaged. To allow it:

1. Double-click OnePaste.app. A dialog says Apple cannot verify the app.
   Click Done.
2. Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security, and scroll down to the
   Security section.
3. Click Open Anyway next to the message about OnePaste, then confirm
   (it may ask for your password or Touch ID).
4. If the message says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", run this in
   Terminal and try again (replace <path> with the folder that contains the app):

       xattr -cr <path>/OnePaste.app

You only have to do this once.

OnePaste is an early beta. It can keep a searchable history of what you copy and,
if you connect it to your own server, sync it between your computers. See the
release page for what works, what does not yet, and how to remove it completely.
